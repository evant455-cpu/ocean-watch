# Russian EEZ, January to April 2022 (first analysis)

Region: GFW `public-eez-areas` id 5690. Data: Global Fishing Watch. No vessel names in this document.

## Results
- **Fishing effort:** 32,271 grid rows, about 551,000 apparent fishing hours. Busiest area: Sea of Okhotsk
  and Pacific coast waters (roughly 41 to 63 N, 130 to 180 E).
- **Radar:** 7,147 SAR detections; 1,826 (25.5%) with no AIS match. Unmatched clusters: Black Sea, Baltic
  (near Kaliningrad), Barents Sea (near Murmansk), and the southern edge of the Pacific fishing grounds.
- **Gaps:** 77 gap events pulled; 19 were short (under 72 h) gaps by fishing vessels flagged by GFW as possible
  intentional disabling. Three vessels (A, B, C) had two short gaps each. Most gaps show the vessel nearly
  stationary while dark; a few travelled 300 to 800 km. Locations cluster in the Sea of Okhotsk fishing grounds.
- **Encounters** for A, B, C: 10 events (8 fishing-carrier, 2 fishing-fishing), all Russian-flagged, ships
  12 to 50 m apart at 0.02 to 1.4 knots (consistent with alongside transfers). Three encounters came 58 to 77
  hours after a gap ended; the rest were a week or more from any gap. GFW's potential-risk flag was False on all
  10. Public-authorization status: both ships authorized in 7, both "not matching" in 3 (status changed by date
  for the same vessels, so this may reflect permit dates or list coverage).

## Interpretation
Consistent with routine domestic fishing and transshipment. Nothing here is evidence of wrongdoing.
